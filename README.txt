MRS TOYIN — READY-MADE BIRTHDAY WEBSITE
========================================

This package contains the three supplied photos and TWO birthday
videos for Mrs Toyin.

VIDEO EXPERIENCE
----------------
- No background audio/music is included or played.
- Both videos are displayed in clean, responsive cards.
- On desktop they sit side-by-side instead of taking the whole page.
- On phones they stack neatly and stay compact.
- Each video has a dedicated fullscreen button that expands the whole
  card into a dark, gold-accented presentation.

MEDIA INCLUDED
--------------
media/photo1.jpg = supplied photo 1
media/photo2.jpg = supplied photo 2
media/photo3.jpg = supplied photo 3
media/video1.mp4 = supplied video 1
media/video2.mp4 = supplied video 2

The gallery uses only the three supplied photos. Photo 1 keeps the
full image visible so it is not awkwardly cropped.

-------------
